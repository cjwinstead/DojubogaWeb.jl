How to play

Place every domino on the board so that each coloured region keeps its rule:

  • 7 its pips add up to 7
  • <4 >9 they add up to less or more
  • = they are all the same
  • ≠ they are all different

Blank squares have no rule. Every puzzle has exactly one solution.

Drag a domino onto the board, or tap it and then tap a square: the ringed half lands there. Rotate with R, right-click, or by tapping the held domino again. Pick a placed domino up by tapping it.

Inspired by Pips from The New York Times Games; Dojuboga is an independent project.
